## Cell storage

Baseline storage-budget regression failed at 50,331,648 bytes for two 1024² grids.
Unused Cell x/y fields existed only in initialization and test helpers. Using
ndarray indices as the position truth reduces Cell 24→8 bytes. Final compiled
harness smoke prints 8 bytes; buffer-budget regression passes. Cell storage for
two grids is 48→16 MiB at 1024², excluding array/allocator/World overhead.
No serialization/public cell representation is currently exported by the app.

## Correctness and reproducibility

384 seeded small fixtures (5/7/16 sides, 64 seeds, fire on/off), 24 ticks each:
per-phase water budgets, finite/nonnegative scalars, sand counts, wood counts
outside the burning phase, unchanged Dirt/borders and equality to the production
schedule all pass. Additional 100/256/512/1024 fixtures run two ticks each.
Original sub-threshold Water cleanup is budgeted explicitly; f32 transfer rounding
has a separate bound. Exact focused tests exposed and fixed water loss when sand,
fire or smoke occupied a None cell with passive water. A 100-side smoke phase lost
0.08 units before the fix; the mixed phase budgets pass afterward.

Native debug control check: selected 1024, clicked Resize and clear, observed both
pan limits reach 1024 and the initial merged border render. Subsequent automated
input returned noWindowsAvailable while the process remained alive; further
native generation/visual checks were not completed. Owned app and wrapper removed.
